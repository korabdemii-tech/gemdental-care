Privacy policy

This page is a draft template prepared for GemDental Care. It must be reviewed and completed by the clinic, ideally with legal advice for its jurisdiction, before the website goes live.

Who we are

This website is operated by GemDental Care [To be confirmed] ("GemDental Care", "we"). For any question about your personal data, contact us at [To be confirmed].

What we collect

When you book an appointment we collect your name, phone number, email address, the treatment and time you choose, whether you have visited before, and any note you add.

We do not ask for medical history through this website. Please do not include detailed health information in the note field.

If you opt in, we also record your consent to receive appointment messages by SMS or WhatsApp, and when you gave it.

For security, our servers process technical data such as your IP address to prevent abuse. We store it only in a one-way, pseudonymised form for rate limiting.

Why we use it

To arrange, confirm, remind you of and manage your appointment, and to contact you about it. The basis for this is taking steps at your request before providing care, and your consent where you opt in to SMS or WhatsApp messages.

Who we share it with

Only with service providers that help us run the booking system on our behalf, such as our hosting provider and the providers that deliver email, SMS or WhatsApp messages. They may only use your data to provide that service. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

How long we keep it

Booking records are kept for 36 months after your last appointment, unless the law requires us to keep them longer, after which they are deleted or anonymised.

Your rights

Depending on the law that applies, you may ask to access, correct or delete your data, to restrict or object to its use, and to withdraw consent for SMS or WhatsApp messages at any time. Contact [To be confirmed]. You may also complain to the data protection authority in your country.

Cookies

This website uses only strictly necessary cookies: one remembers your language choice, and staff sign-in uses a security cookie. We do not use analytics or advertising cookies.
