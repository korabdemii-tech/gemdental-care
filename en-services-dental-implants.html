Dental implants

A long-term option for replacing one or more missing teeth.

Illustration

What to expect

A dental implant replaces the root of a missing tooth and supports a crown, bridge or denture on top. Treatment happens in stages over several months.

The first appointment is an assessment: the dentist examines the area, discusses whether an implant is suitable for you, and outlines the steps, timeline and costs involved.

Every mouth is different. The dentist will confirm the right treatment for you after an examination.
