From booking to your visit

Five simple steps, all of them online.

  1. 01

    Choose your treatment

    Pick a treatment, or a consultation if you're not sure what you need.

  2. 02

    Find an available time

    Only real, open times are shown, updated as others book.

  3. 03

    Enter your details

    Your name, phone and email. No medical history needed online.

  4. 04

    Receive confirmation

    You'll see clearly whether your visit is confirmed or awaiting approval.

  5. 05

    Visit GemDental

    Arrive a few minutes early and we'll take it from there.

Questions, answered

Still have a question?

Get in touch

Choose a treatment, pick a day and a time that shows as available, and enter your contact details. The whole process takes a couple of minutes, and you'll receive a reference number at the end.

Book a consultation. It's the right choice whenever you're unsure: the dentist will examine your teeth and explain the options before anything else happens.

The last step of the booking tells you clearly whether your appointment is confirmed or whether it's a request waiting for the clinic's approval. If it's a request, you'll receive a separate message once it has been confirmed.
